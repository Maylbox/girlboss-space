Opening the card room…

Fictional chips. No cash value.

Table rules

  • Six decks. Dealer stands on soft 17 and peeks for Blackjack.
  • Blackjack pays 3:2. An ordinary win pays 1:1. A tie returns your bet.
  • Double on any first two cards, including after a split.
  • Split matching ranks, up to three hands. Split aces get one card each, then stand. A split 21 is not a natural Blackjack.
  • No insurance or surrender.
  • Start with 1,000 chips. Bet 10–250, in steps of 10.
  • Shoe reshuffles before a round at 25% remaining. If a very long round exhausts the shoe, a fresh shuffled shoe is used.
  • The host starts and advances rounds. A player who cannot afford the minimum sits out. Turns stand after 60 seconds.
  • Seats are held for 90 seconds after disconnecting. Tables expire after 24 hours without activity.
